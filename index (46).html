Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information 3D Hub collects when you visit this website or order from us, and what we do with it. We are a small online shop based in Dubai, UAE, and we try to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

This website has no accounts or tracking tools. The only time we receive your personal information is when you give it to us yourself:

  • When you place an order, you fill in our checkout form with your name, email address, phone number and delivery address. We receive these details together with your order.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write in your message.

How we use it

We use your details only to confirm and deliver your order, to answer your questions, to handle exchanges or problems with an order, and to keep basic records of our sales. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Your consent

Before you can place an order you tick a box to agree to our Terms & Conditions, our Refund Policy and this Privacy Policy, and to the details you enter in the checkout form being sent to us. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us. We may still need to keep records of orders we have already delivered.

Who we share it with

We only share what is needed to get your order to you, for example your name, phone number and address with whoever delivers it. We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

Some services we rely on handle data as well. Our website host, Netlify, receives your checkout form and passes it on to us by email, and our email is provided by Google. If you message us on WhatsApp, Meta carries that message under its own privacy policy. This website also loads its fonts from Google and some of its animation scripts from jsDelivr, so when you visit, your device connects to their servers and they can see your IP address. Some of these services may process information outside the UAE.

Cookies and storage

We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools on this website. You can read more in our Cookie Policy. Your cart is kept only in your browser while the page is open, and it is emptied once you send your order.

How long we keep it

We keep orders only for as long as we need them to deliver your order, deal with any exchange or complaint, and keep our sales records. After that we delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw your consent. Email us at The3DHubUAE@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can. We may need to keep some information if the law requires us to.

Security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe, including keeping orders in our private email account and the form inbox of our website host. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Children

Our products may appeal to children, but orders should be placed by an adult. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may change this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
